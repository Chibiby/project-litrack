# ARAL System Training Videos — Recording Script (v2)

Version 2, corrected against the app on `main`. Every screen name, button label and field list
below was checked in the code; what changed from v1 and why is in `script-app-audit.md`.

**Three modules.** What was one long teachers-and-school-heads module is split at the seam
between the School Head's one-time setup and the teacher's ongoing job, so nobody watches
thirteen minutes that are not theirs.

| | Audience | Parts | Narration | With holds |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Module 1 — For School Heads** | School heads, once per school | 7 | 9:58 | ~12–14 min |
| **Module 2 — For Teachers** | Every DepEd teacher | 9 | 12:22 | ~15–17 min |
| **Module 3 — For Non-DepEd ARAL Volunteers** | Volunteer tutors, and the teachers who assign to them | 7 | 6:34 | ~8–9 min |

Each module stands alone. Module 2 and Module 3 both re-teach opening the site and choosing the
district and school, because a teacher who watches only their own module has not seen Module 1.
Module 2 also says up front that the Teachers button stays greyed out until the school head
finishes Module 1, which is the single most likely thing to stop a teacher on day one.

**Legend:**
`[SCREEN]` = what you do in OBS · **"Voiceover"** = what you say · `⏸` = pause point for editing

---

## BEFORE YOU RECORD

- Use demo accounts only. No real learner names or grades on screen.
- Turn OFF password manager, browser autofill, notifications, and any browser extensions.
- Browser at 1920×1080, zoom 100%. Never resize mid-recording.
- Record each **Part** as a separate OBS take. Short takes are easier to re-do than one long one.
- Move the mouse slowly and deliberately. Pause 2 seconds on a button *before* clicking it.
- **Site address: `arallitrack.com`** — always shown typed into the address bar, never reached
  from a bookmark or search. Clear the address bar of autocomplete history first, or the URL will
  fill itself in before you finish typing and viewers will miss it.
- The app says **Sign out**, not Log out. Say "sign out" throughout.
- Sample data used throughout:
  - District: **[your district]** · School: **[your school]** · School ID: **[e.g. 123456]**
  - School Head: **Maria Santos** · Teacher: **Juan dela Cruz**
  - Volunteer: **Rosa Mendoza** · Section: **Grade 7 – Sampaguita**
  - Learners: **Ana Reyes, Ben Cruz, Carla Lim**
