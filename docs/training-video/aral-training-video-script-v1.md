# ARAL System Training Videos — Recording Script

**Two modules:**
- **Module 1 — For Teachers and School Heads** (est. 11–13 min)
- **Module 2 — For Non-DepEd ARAL Volunteers** (est. 6–7 min)

**Legend:**
`[SCREEN]` = what you do in OBS · **"Voiceover"** = what you say · `⏸` = pause point for editing

---

## BEFORE YOU RECORD

- Use demo accounts only. No real learner names, LRNs, or grades on screen.
- Turn OFF password manager, browser autofill, notifications, and any browser extensions.
- Browser at 1920×1080, zoom 100%. Never resize mid-recording.
- Record each **Part** as a separate OBS take. Short takes are easier to re-do than one long one.
- Move the mouse slowly and deliberately. Pause 2 seconds on a button *before* clicking it.
- **Site address: `arallitrack.com`** — always shown typed into the address bar, never reached from a bookmark or search. Clear the address bar of autocomplete history first, or the URL will fill itself in before you finish typing and viewers will miss it.
- Sample data used throughout:
  - District: **[your district]** · School: **[your school]** · School ID: **[e.g. 123456]**
  - School Head: **Maria Santos** · Teacher: **Juan dela Cruz**
  - Volunteer: **Rosa Mendoza** · Section: **Grade 7 – Sampaguita**
  - Learners: **Ana Reyes, Ben Cruz, Carla Lim**

## EDITING NOTES

**Pacing.** Read slower than feels natural. Aim for about 120–130 words per minute. The most common failure in training videos is narration that outruns the viewer's eyes.

**Zooms.** Zoom in on: the district and school dropdowns, the password field on first login, the ARAL toggle, and the tutor dropdown. These are the four places people get stuck.

**Callouts.** Put a highlight ring or arrow on every button *before* you click it, not after.

**Speed-ups.** Parts 8 and 10 in Module 1, and Part 3 in Module 2, can run at 2x with the music raised — they are repetition, not new learning.

**Music.** Upbeat at the intro, transitions, and outro. Drop to 10–15% during all instruction. Fade out completely during Part 3 and Part 11 of Module 1 — those are the two parts people will rewatch.

**Captions.** Burn in captions if you can. Faculty rooms are noisy and many teachers will watch on a phone without earphones.

**Chapters.** When you upload, add chapter markers using the Part names. A teacher who forgot one step should not have to scrub through twelve minutes.
